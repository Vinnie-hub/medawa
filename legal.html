Terms of use

Unopened, non-prescription items can be returned within 7 days. Refunds go to your original payment method.

Prescription-only medicines are dispensed only after a Medawa pharmacist has verified a valid prescription. We may contact you or your prescriber to confirm details, and can decline an order that fails the check.
