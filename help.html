Help & FAQs

Quick answers about ordering, delivery, prescriptions and returns.

How does ordering work?

Browse products, add them to your cart, enter your delivery details and choose a payment method. Medawa then processes your order, with any required prescription and pharmacist checks, and delivers it to you.

How long does delivery take?

Same-day in Nairobi for orders before 3pm; 1 to 3 days elsewhere. Delivery is free above KES 2,500.

Do I need a prescription?

Prescription-only medicines (marked Rx) need a valid prescription, which our pharmacists verify before dispatch.

How do I pay?

M-Pesa, card, or cash on delivery.

Can I return an item?

Unopened, non-prescription items can be returned within 7 days.

Who supplies the products?

Medawa is a single online pharmacy, not a marketplace. All products are sourced, checked and dispatched by Medawa.
